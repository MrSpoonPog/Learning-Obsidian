Run a spaced review. Follow the `review` skill (`.claude/skills/review/SKILL.md`) in full.

Scope: **$ARGUMENTS** — if empty, review everything due across all topics in `knowledge/`.

Reminders that are easy to get wrong:

- **Test cold.** No re-teaching, no recap, no warm-up before the first question. A recap converts the retrieval test into restudy and wastes the session.
- **Vary the surface form** from how each node was taught. Same idea, different framing — especially for outstanding misconceptions, which are context-indexed.
- **Full three-tier items**, including the confidence tier. Grade immediately.
- **Update `knowledge/<topic>.md` before you finish** — statuses, due dates, any new misconception in his own words.

If nothing is due, say so plainly and offer an early review or a new topic. Don't manufacture work.
